Category · Year

ESP32 Hand Gesture PC Controller

Designed and developed a gesture-controlled interface that allows users to operate a computer without a mouse. Using an ESP32-CAM and computer vision algorithms, the system recognizes specific hand gestures and translates them into real-time cursor movement, clicks, and scrolling actions. This project explored the intersection of embedded systems, artificial intelligence, and human-computer interaction while demonstrating potential applications in accessibility and touch-free computing.

03
Category · Year

Smart Note Digitization System

Designed and developed a note digitization system that streamlines the process of organizing handwritten study materials. Users can upload photos of their notes from a mobile phone to a website hosted on a local network, where they are automatically categorized by subject and date. The system generates placeholders for missing submissions and compiles all uploaded notes into subject-wise PDF documents. By combining web technologies, automation, and document processing, the project simplifies note management and creates a structured digital archive of academic work.
